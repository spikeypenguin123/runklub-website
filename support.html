RunKlub Support

We're a small Australian team. We aim to reply within 2 business days, and to safety reports within 24 hours.

Contact us

Email runklub99@gmail.com. Please include your display name and the iPhone and iOS version you're using. For bugs, you can also use Report a bug inside the app, which attaches the app version for you.

In an emergency, call 000.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse the map, clubs and events as a guest. You need a free account (16+) to join or create clubs and events, add friends and use event chat.

How do I sign in?

With your phone number (SMS code), Google, Apple or email and password.

Why can't I see anything near me?

RunKlub is new and launching in Australia first, starting on the Gold Coast. Check location access is on (iOS Settings → RunKlub → Location → While Using), zoom out on the map, or clear the activity and day filters. You can also create an event yourself.

Is my location shared?

No. Your location is only used to show nearby sessions and send nearby notifications. Other members never see it. Meeting points are public landmarks.

How do I create an event?

Tap the + button on the map, or press and hold on the map where you want to meet. Choose a public landmark, never a home address.

How do I change notifications?

Profile → Settings to change the radius or turn them off, or iOS Settings → Notifications → RunKlub.

How old do I need to be?

16 or older. We check your date of birth at sign-up and keep only the year.

How to report someone or something

  • A member: open their profile and tap Report. Tap Block to stop seeing them (manage blocks in Profile → Settings → Blocked users).
  • A club: open the club, tap …, then Report.
  • An event: open the event and tap Report Event.
  • A chat message: press and hold the message, then tap Report.

We review reports within 24 hours. See our Community Guidelines for what we remove.

How to delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This deletes your account and data. Full details: Delete your account.
