Delete your RunKlub account

You can delete your account at any time, for free, from inside the app.

In the app

  1. Open RunKlub and tap the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account, then confirm.
  4. If you signed in a while ago you'll be asked to sign in again (SMS code, Google, Apple or password). This protects you from someone else deleting your account.

That's it. You'll be signed out and returned to guest browsing.

Can't get into the app?

Email runklub99@gmail.com from the email address on your account (or tell us the phone number you signed in with) and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm it's you and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email, phone number, Google or Apple link). If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke RunKlub's access to your Apple ID.
  • Your profile: name, bio, paces, interests, languages, sex, ethnicity, Instagram handle, audio intro and photos.
  • Your private record: email, location, birth year and age-check result, block list, notification settings and push token.
  • Clubs and events you created, and your memberships and RSVPs in other clubs and events.
  • Your event chat messages.
  • Your session ratings, check-ins and survey answers (only anonymous totals remain).
  • Friends and friend requests, and notifications sent to you.
  • Feedback you sent, and its attachments.

Deletion normally completes within minutes. Backups roll off within 30 days.

What we keep, and why

  • Reports (made by you or about you), including a copy of reported content, for up to 12 months for safety and legal reasons.
  • Security audit logs (user IDs and actions, not content) for up to 12 months.
  • Usage statistics, which are tied to a random install ID rather than your account, until they expire after 90 days.
  • Crash reports that aren't linked to your name, for up to 90 days.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. See our Privacy Policy for more.
