RunKlub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026. Applies to the RunKlub iOS app and this website.

RunKlub helps you find local run clubs and group sessions. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how and why we use it, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. We keep it short and plain on purpose. If anything is unclear, email runklub99@gmail.com.

"RunKlub", "we" and "us" means Tom Bannerman, based in Queensland, Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), whether or not a small-business exemption applies to us.

1. The short version

  • You can browse the map, clubs and events without an account.
  • We never show your location to anyone. Meeting points are always public landmarks, never home addresses.
  • Your email, phone number, location and birth year are private to you. We never store your full date of birth.
  • What you put on your profile is visible to other signed-in RunKlub members.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and data in the app at any time: Profile → Settings → Delete Account.

3. Location

If you allow it, the app uses your device location while it is open. We store your latest position and a geohash in a private record only you can read. Our servers use it to decide which nearby clubs and events to notify you about, within the radius you choose (1 to 50 km). Other members never see your location. Meeting points for clubs and events must be public places such as parks, beaches or cafes, and the app warns you never to use a home address. You can turn location off in iOS Settings and still browse.

4. Who processes your data (third parties)

We only share personal information with service providers that process it on our behalf under contracts that require them to protect it at least as well as this policy does:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in (Firebase Authentication), database (Cloud Firestore), file storage (Cloud Storage), notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), backend functions (Cloud Functions), crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) and abuse protection (App Check). Our database and files are hosted in Google Cloud's australia-southeast1 (Sydney) region; some Firebase services (for example Crashlytics and Cloud Messaging) process data in other countries, including the United States.
  • Apple: Apple Maps (MapKit) shows maps and place search; Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications; Sign in with Apple if you use it.
  • Google Sign-In, if you use it.
  • GitHub (GitHub Inc., United States): bug reports you send through the app may be copied into our private issue tracker, without your email or phone number.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it (for example, a valid request from police or the eSafety Commissioner) or to protect someone's safety. We do not sell or rent personal information and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. You may be asked to sign in again to confirm. This permanently deletes your sign-in account and, automatically on our servers, your profile, private record (email, location, birth year, block list, notification settings), audio intro and photos, the clubs and events you created, your memberships and RSVPs, your chat messages, your session ratings, check-ins, survey answers and RSVP history, your friends and friend requests, notifications sent to you, and your feedback and attachments. Usage statistics can't be linked to your account, so they aren't affected; they expire after 90 days. Deletion normally completes within minutes; backups roll off within 30 days. Reports kept for safety are handled as described in section 5. See Delete your account for details, or email us to request deletion if you can no longer sign in.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: edit your profile any time in the app, or email us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you. We respond within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent: remove optional details (such as ethnicity or your audio intro) in Edit Profile, turn off location or notifications in iOS Settings, or delete your account.
  • Usage statistics: turn them off at any time in Profile → Settings. The app keeps working exactly the same.
  • Notifications: change the radius or turn them off in Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Block and report: block anyone from their profile; report members, clubs, events and chat messages.
  • Marketing: we don't send marketing emails or SMS. If we ever do, we will ask first and every message will have an unsubscribe link.
  • Complain: contact us first at runklub99@gmail.com. If you're not satisfied with our response within 30 days, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

8. Age

RunKlub is for people aged 16 and over. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up (and again if we have no age check on record for your account) and don't allow anyone younger to use an account. The app checks your age to the day on your phone from the full date you enter; we don't store the full date, only your birth year and that you passed. If the date you enter makes you under 16, the app first shows the date back to you and asks you to confirm it, so a slip of the date wheel can be fixed. If you confirm it, we don't create an account (email sign-up), or we sign you out and delete the account (other sign-in methods). If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we delete it. If you are under 18, please use RunKlub with a parent or guardian's knowledge, and only meet people at public, supervised sessions.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Cloud. Access is restricted by database security rules (for example, your private record can only be read by you) and by App Check. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the OAIC as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect and update the date above.
