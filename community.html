Community Guidelines

Last updated: 29 September 2026. These guidelines are part of our Terms of Use.

RunKlub exists to help people find local clubs and training buddies and meet up safely. Everyone, whatever their pace, should feel welcome. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive members.

Be a good club mate

  • Be respectful and inclusive. All paces, bodies and backgrounds are welcome.
  • Keep listings honest: real times, real distances, real paces, a real public meeting point.
  • Keep event chat about the session.
  • Respect "no". If someone doesn't reply, doesn't want to run with you, or blocks you, leave it there.

What gets removed

We remove the content, and may suspend or permanently remove the account, for:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, stalking, or unwanted romantic or sexual attention.
  • Hate speech or discrimination.
  • Sexual or nude content, and anything that sexualises minors (reported to police).
  • Violence, weapons, self-harm or eating-disorder promotion, illegal drugs or other illegal activity.
  • Sharing anyone's home address, live location, phone number or other private details without consent.
  • Meeting points at private homes.
  • Spam, scams, fake clubs or events, pyramid schemes and unauthorised advertising.
  • Impersonating a person, club or business.
  • Under-16 accounts.

How to report

  • A member: open their profile and tap Report. Tap Block to stop seeing them.
  • A club: open the club, tap the … menu, then Report.
  • An event: open the event and tap Report Event.
  • A chat message: press and hold the message, then tap Report.
  • Or email runklub99@gmail.com with a screenshot if you can.

Reports are confidential: the person you report isn't told who reported them. We review reports within 24 hours. If you've reported serious online abuse and it hasn't been dealt with, you can also report it to the eSafety Commissioner. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 000.

Safety tips for meeting in person

  • Meet at the public landmark in the listing, in daylight or a well-lit area, and preferably with a group.
  • Tell a friend or family member where you're going and when you'll be back.
  • Don't share your home address or live location in your profile or chat, and don't get in a stranger's car.
  • Run at a pace and distance you're comfortable with. Carry water and your phone, and check the weather, tides and surf conditions for beach or ocean sessions.
  • Swim only between the flags or with a qualified squad; ride with lights and a helmet.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels off, leave, block, and report.
  • If you're under 18, let a parent or guardian know and stick to public group sessions.
